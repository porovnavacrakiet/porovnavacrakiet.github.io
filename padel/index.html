EQUIPMENT NOTE

Designed around official padel rules.

FIP Rules of Padel require rackets made in accordance with its racket-homologation annex, including limits for total length, width and thickness, a perforated hitting surface and a wrist cord. FIP's public certification page lists approved balls, not an individual public list of approved rackets; catalogue values here are practical comparison estimates, not a claim of FIP product approval.
