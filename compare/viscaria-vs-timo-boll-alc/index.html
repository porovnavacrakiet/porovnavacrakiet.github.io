Viscaria vs
Timo Boll ALC

Two classic outer-ALC blades, two benchmark offensive rubbers, and one useful question: which difference will you actually feel at the table?

BLADE: BUTTERFLY VISCARIA VS TIMO BOLL ALC · RUBBER: TENERGY 05 VS DIGNICS 05

SHORT ANSWER

Choose the handle and rubber response you trust.

  • The blades share the same 5+2 outer Arylate-Carbon construction and 5.8 mm thickness.
  • Viscaria and Timo Boll ALC are close enough that handle shape, weight and rubbers matter more than a headline speed rating.
  • Tenergy 05 is the more familiar, elastic reference; Dignics 05 feels firmer and gives a longer, deeper trajectory on committed strokes.

There is no published worldwide sales ranking that can honestly prove the exact two most-used blades or rubbers. Viscaria, Timo Boll ALC and Tenergy 05 are long-running reference products used from ambitious clubs to elite play; Dignics 05 is a widely used modern comparison point. This guide treats them as benchmarks, not as an invented global ranking.

What is actually the same?

Both blades are offensive shakehand designs built with five wood plies and two outer Arylate-Carbon layers. Butterfly lists both at 157 × 150 mm and 5.8 mm thick, with the same speed and reaction rating in its current specification table. In other words, neither is a meaningful shortcut to a completely different category of blade.

Both rubbers use pimple code No. 05 and are made for topspin-oriented play. The more useful comparison is how their sponge and topsheet change the timing, contact and trajectory of that topspin.

Viscaria vs Timo Boll ALC: the practical difference

For most players, the first difference is the handle. The Viscaria FL handle is listed at 100 × 25 × 34 mm, while the Timo Boll ALC FL is 100 × 24 × 34 mm. A millimetre sounds trivial, but the hand notices it: Viscaria often feels a touch fuller in the palm, while Timo Boll ALC can feel slightly more compact. Handle preference is not cosmetic; it changes how freely you relax, switch to the backhand and hold the racket on serve receive.

Individual weight and balance can create a larger difference than the model name. Two examples of the same blade can differ by several grams. If possible, choose a measured weight rather than assuming every Viscaria or Timo Boll ALC will swing identically.

BLADE VERDICT

Do not replace one with the other for a dramatic speed change.

  • Choose Viscaria if its fuller FL handle feels naturally secure and you want the classic ALC reference.
  • Choose Timo Boll ALC if you prefer its slightly narrower handle profile or already like its balance in your hand.
  • Keep your current blade if it fits your hand: a rubber change is usually the more noticeable and lower-risk adjustment.

Tenergy 05 vs Dignics 05

Tenergy 05 is the classic high-arc topspin rubber: it feels elastic on brush contact and gives clear feedback when opening against backspin. It remains a dependable choice for players who build rallies with spin, change direction and value an intuitive first opening.

Dignics 05 uses Spring Sponge X and a more durable, grippier topsheet. Compared with Tenergy 05, it feels firmer and more solid under a strong stroke. Butterfly describes its trajectory as higher and longer, with a deeper landing ball and a harder kick. That can reward an active forehand or counter-topspin, but it also asks for more precise timing and a more confident touch in the short game.

Neither rubber is automatically “better.” Tenergy 05 gives a lively, familiar release; Dignics 05 rewards a more committed contact with stability and depth. If you struggle to control the return with either, changing thickness or using it on just one side is often wiser than putting the hardest option on both sides immediately.

Four sensible starting setups

STARTING POINTS, NOT RULES

  • Viscaria + Tenergy 05 FH/BH: for the player who wants a familiar, spin-first offensive reference.
  • Timo Boll ALC + Tenergy 05 FH/BH: the same broad offensive direction, with the deciding factor being handle comfort.
  • Viscaria + Dignics 05 FH, Tenergy 05 BH: more depth and stability on the forehand while keeping a more elastic backhand opening.
  • Timo Boll ALC + Dignics 05 FH/BH: for an advanced player who actively counterattacks and already likes firmer contact.

Who should choose which?

Choose by the shot you want to improve, not by the professional name on the handle. A club player working on a safer opening against backspin will often learn faster with the rubber that makes contact feel predictable. A player who already counterloops from mid-distance may benefit more from the firmer, deeper response of Dignics 05. In both cases, a sound technique and a comfortable handle remain more important than chasing a small specification difference.

Method and sources

This page combines the product construction and dimensions published by Butterfly with the normalized catalogue values used in Racket Comparator. “Feel”, forgiveness and suitability are practical guidance rather than laboratory measurements, and weight, glue, thickness and individual technique can change the result.
