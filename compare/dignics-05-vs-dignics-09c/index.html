RUBBER COMPARISON · UPDATED AUGUST 2026

Dignics 05 vs
Dignics 09C

Two hard, high-performance Dignics options: one more traditionally grippy and direct, the other tackier and built for a more deliberate forehand contact.

BUTTERFLY · SPRING SPONGE X · TOPSPIN RUBBERS

SHORT ANSWER

05 is the versatile grippy choice; 09C adds tack and demands more acceleration.

  • Dignics 05 suits direct topspin and counter-topspin on both wings.
  • Dignics 09C creates a more tacky, deliberate forehand contact and strong spin potential.
  • For many players, 09C on FH and 05 on BH is the sensible starting test.

Dignics 05 and Dignics 09C share a premium, firm Spring Sponge X platform, but their topsheets point the player toward different contacts. Dignics 05 is a grippy, comparatively direct modern topspin rubber. Dignics 09C adds tack and a higher demand for purposeful acceleration, especially when lifting a low ball.

Backhand and counters: Dignics 05 activates faster and is the more natural choice for early, direct play.

Forehand first ball: 09C’s higher spin and much firmer, tackier profile suit a deliberate, accelerating contact rather than a passive touch.

Dignics 05: direct grip and countering

Dignics 05 offers strong mechanical grip with a lively, firm response. It suits a player who wants to take the ball early, counter over the table and use the backhand actively. On the forehand it gives a clear, reliable route from opening topspin to a second faster topspin without needing a Chinese-style swing.

Dignics 09C: tackier and more deliberate

Dignics 09C’s tackier surface can help a player load spin on service and on a committed opening. It rewards a full, accelerating forehand contact and can feel very secure once the stroke is engaged. The cost is that a passive touch, rushed backhand or half-committed opening may need more adjustment than with Dignics 05.

RUBBER VERDICT

Assign each rubber to the job you actually play.

  • Choose Dignics 05 for a versatile attack, active backhand and direct countering.
  • Choose Dignics 09C for a forehand-led game with deliberate acceleration and tackier service/first topspin feel.
  • Try 09C FH + 05 BH before moving to 09C on both sides; it keeps the backhand faster to activate.

Do not ignore blade and thickness

A fast outer-carbon blade can make both rubbers feel demanding. A softer or inner-fibre blade may give 09C more dwell, while 05 remains quicker to activate. Thickness also matters: changing it is often a smaller, safer adjustment than changing both the blade and rubber family at once.
