BLADE COMPARISON · UPDATED AUGUST 2026

Viscaria vs
Innerforce Layer ALC

The classic outer-versus-inner ALC choice: a crisper, earlier response against a softer feel with more time on the ball.

BUTTERFLY · ARYLATE-CARBON · OFFENSIVE SHAKEHAND

SHORT ANSWER

Choose where you want to feel the composite.

  • Viscaria places ALC nearer the surface for a quicker, crisper response.
  • Innerforce Layer ALC places it deeper for more dwell and a softer first touch.
  • Neither is “better”; the choice follows your timing and preferred contact.

These blades solve a similar problem in different ways: both provide the stability of Arylate-Carbon, but their layer placement changes when that stability becomes noticeable. Viscaria tends to reward a direct active stroke. Innerforce Layer ALC gives a little more sense of holding the ball before it leaves the racket.

Outer ALC: Viscaria

With the composite layer near the outer ply, Viscaria has a crisp, immediate response. It is a natural fit for players who counter at the table, punch the backhand and want the racket to return energy clearly on a short stroke. The trade-off is that passive contacts and delicate receive require a calm hand and precise racket angle.

Inner ALC: Innerforce Layer ALC

Innerforce Layer ALC places the composite closer to the core, behind more wood. The contact tends to feel softer and more flexible, especially at lower impact. That can make opening against backspin, short touch and spin variation feel more intuitive, while the composite still offers stability when the stroke becomes stronger.

PRACTICAL VERDICT

Choose the response that matches your default timing.

  • Choose Viscaria for direct blocks, active counters and a crisp outer-fibre reference.
  • Choose Innerforce Layer ALC if you value dwell, a softer opening feel and more feedback in the short game.
  • Do not choose only by speed: rubber hardness and your actual blade weight can narrow or widen the gap.

Who should change?

A player who often feels rushed with an outer-ALC blade may prefer the extra feedback of Innerforce Layer ALC. A player whose inner-fibre blade feels too soft or delayed on counters may appreciate Viscaria’s earlier release. Use identical rubbers and comparable weights when testing, otherwise the change in setup will hide the blade difference.

Method and sources

This guide combines Butterfly’s published construction information with the normalized catalogue values in Racket Comparator. It is practical guidance, not a laboratory claim. Check Butterfly’s current blade specifications before buying.
