RUBBER COMPARISON · UPDATED AUGUST 2026

Tenergy 05 vs
Dignics 05

Two benchmark 05 rubbers with a shared topspin purpose but a clearly different sponge, impact feel and demand on the player.

BUTTERFLY · PIMPLE CODE NO. 05 · TOPSPIN RUBBERS

SHORT ANSWER

Tenergy is the elastic classic; Dignics is the firmer modern option.

  • Tenergy 05 feels lively and intuitive on a brushing opening.
  • Dignics 05 has a firmer, grippier response and rewards stronger contact.
  • Choose by the shot you want to improve, not by a promise of a universal upgrade.

Both rubbers are designed around the No. 05 pimple geometry and suit spin-oriented attacking play. The key decision is not the number printed on the packet. It is whether you prefer Tenergy 05’s elastic release and familiar feedback, or Dignics 05’s firmer, more stable response when the ball is struck hard.

Opening topspin: Tenergy’s softer, elastic feel is usually easier to activate with a compact brushing contact.

Counter-topspin: Dignics’ firmer, faster profile rewards a committed stroke and gives more penetration when the rally speeds up.

Tenergy 05: elastic and direct

Tenergy 05 is the established reference for a high-arc topspin feel. It activates readily on a brushing contact and gives clear feedback when opening against backspin. Players who build rallies through spin, use a compact backhand topspin or value a familiar springy response often adapt to it quickly.

Dignics 05: firmer and more committed

Dignics 05 uses Spring Sponge X and a grippier topsheet. It feels firmer under pressure and can give a deeper, more solid ball when you accelerate through the contact. That stability suits counter-topspin and active play, but a relaxed or passive touch can feel less forgiving until the player adjusts timing and racket angle.

RUBBER VERDICT

Start with the side of the racket that exposes your priority.

  • Choose Tenergy 05 for an elastic, spin-first reference and easier familiar activation.
  • Choose Dignics 05 for firmer contact, strong counters and a more committed trajectory.
  • Mix them if you want Dignics 05 on the forehand and a livelier, easier-opening backhand.

Thickness and blade change the answer

A 1.9 mm sheet can make either rubber calmer than a 2.1 mm sheet, while a fast outer-carbon blade makes the difference feel more immediate. Change one variable at a time if possible. The same rubber can feel entirely different on a soft all-wood blade and on a stiff composite blade.
